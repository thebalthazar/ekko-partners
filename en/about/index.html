About Ekko

Specialists in the real economy.

A boutique capital markets firm built to serve agribusiness and real estate with depth, independence and end-to-end execution.

Why we exist

The right capital, for those who produce.

Agribusiness and real estate are among Brazil's largest economic engines — and yet they often access capital on terms below their potential. Ekko Partners was born to close that gap.

We translate real assets into efficient capital markets instruments, combining the technical rigor of a structurer with the closeness of a long-term partner. We don't sell off-the-shelf products: we design the right transaction for each goal.
