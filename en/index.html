No financial jargon

In practical terms: what does this mean for your business?

"Capital markets" sounds complex — but the outcome is simple: money to get your project off the ground, grow, or unlock cash, without relying on the bank alone.

Need funding for a project?

If you have an agribusiness or real estate project and need capital to execute it, we structure the fundraising with investors — often at better cost and terms than bank credit.

Want to turn what you're owed into cash today?

Contracts, rents, installment sales and agri receivables can become money now. That's receivables advancing, structured as a CRA or CRI.

Have land, crops or property to monetize?

We turn real assets into a fund (FIAGRO or FII) that raises capital from investors and can generate income — while you stay in command of the operation.

We work on structured transactions typically from R$ 5 million. If your project is at that scale, let's talk.
