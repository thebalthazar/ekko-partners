CRA: efficient funding for agribusiness.

We structure Agribusiness Receivables Certificates to fund producers, cooperatives and agro-industries — with competitive cost, longer tenors and income tax exemption for individual investors.

Structure a CRA
What it is

Agri receivables, in the capital markets.

The CRA is a fixed-income security issued by a securitization company and backed by agribusiness credit rights. It lets sector companies advance future receivables and raise funds directly from investors.

With income tax exemption for individual investors, the CRA combines robust demand and competitive funding cost — a powerful alternative to traditional bank credit.

Who it fits

  • Agro-industries and tradersseeking medium- and long-term funding.
  • Cooperativeslooking to diversify funding sources.
  • Input and distribution companieswith a granular receivables portfolio.
  • Large producerswith a structurable receivables flow.

FAQ

CRA in brief

What is a CRA?

A fixed-income security issued by a securitization company, backed by agribusiness receivables, that enables fundraising by advancing future flows.

Is it tax-exempt?

Yes, for individual investors — which increases demand and reduces the issuer's funding cost.

CRA vs CPR?

The CPR is the producer's origination note (possible collateral); the CRA is the securitization security distributed to investors in capital markets.
