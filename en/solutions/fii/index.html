FII: income and capital for real estate.

We structure Real Estate Investment Funds — equity, credit, development or hybrid — to monetize developments and originate recurring income with investor access.

Structure an FII
What it is

The established vehicle for real estate income.

The FII pools investor resources to invest in real estate assets — physical properties, receivables (CRI), development projects or shares of other funds. It is the instrument that popularized real estate investing in Brazil.

For developers, owners and managers, the FII is a way to monetize assets, finance projects and create liquidity — with capital markets governance and transparency.

Who it fits

  • Developers and land subdividerswanting to finance development with market capital.
  • Owners of income propertieslooking to monetize via sale & leaseback or contribution.
  • Managers and originatorswho want to launch a listed vehicle.
  • Investorsseeking recurring and potentially tax-exempt real estate income.

FAQ

FII in brief

What is an FII?

A Real Estate Investment Fund — a vehicle that pools resources to invest in physical properties, receivables (CRI), development or shares of other funds.

What are the types?

Income (equity), credit (receivables), development and fund of funds — plus hybrids that combine strategies.

Is income tax-exempt?

It may be exempt for individual investors, provided the fund is listed and meets legal requirements (minimum holders and exchange trading).
