FIAGRO: agribusiness in the capital markets.

We structure and manage Investment Funds for Agro-Industrial Production Chains across all three types — turning land, crops and receivables into productive capital.

Structure a FIAGRO
What it is

A vehicle designed for agribusiness.

Created by Law 14,130/2021, FIAGRO decisively brought agribusiness closer to capital markets. It is a fund that can invest in rural real estate, agri credit rights and equity in sector companies — with governance, potential liquidity and tax efficiency.

For farmers, cooperatives and agro-industries, it is a way to monetize assets and raise capital. For investors, it offers qualified access to one of the country's most resilient economies.

Who it fits

  • Producers and agro-industriesseeking funding beyond bank credit.
  • Landownerslooking to monetize or appreciate the rural asset.
  • Receivables originators(CPR, CRA) wanting their own vehicle.
  • Qualified investorsseeking agri exposure with fund governance.
Types

Three structures, one goal

We choose the type — or combination — best suited to the transaction's thesis.

Real Estate

FIAGRO-Real Estate

Invests in rural real estate and related rights. Ideal for land monetization, rural sale & leaseback and agricultural development.

FIDC

FIAGRO-Credit Rights

Invests in agribusiness receivables — CPR, CRA and other credit rights. Structured funding for the whole chain.

Equity

FIAGRO-Equity

Invests in equity of agro-industrial companies. Capital for sector growth and consolidation.

FAQ

FIAGRO in brief

What is a FIAGRO?

The Investment Fund for Agro-Industrial Production Chains, created by Law 14,130/2021 to finance agribusiness via capital markets, investing in rural real estate, credit rights and equity.

What are the types?

FIAGRO-Real Estate, FIAGRO-Credit Rights (FIDC) and FIAGRO-Equity (FIP) — which can be combined per the fund's bylaws.

Is there a tax advantage?

Under conditions set by law, income from a listed FIAGRO may be exempt from income tax for individual investors, increasing the vehicle's appeal.
