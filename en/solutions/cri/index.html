CRI: capital for real estate.

We structure Real Estate Receivables Certificates to advance cash flows, finance development and enable leasing and built-to-suit operations — with income tax exemption for individual investors.

Structure a CRI
What it is

Real estate receivables, turned into capital.

The CRI is a fixed-income security issued by a securitization company and backed by real estate credits — financings, rents and purchase-and-sale contracts. It lets issuers advance future flows and finance the sector without relying solely on bank credit.

With income tax exemption for individual investors, the CRI has become one of the pillars of real estate fixed income in Brazil — with strong demand and competitive cost for the issuer.

Who it fits

  • Developersseeking funding for launches and construction.
  • Owners of income propertieswith long-term lease contracts.
  • Built-to-suit and sale & leasebackoperations that generate structurable receivables.
  • Companies with a real estate portfoliolooking to advance flows.

FAQ

CRI in brief

What is a CRI?

A fixed-income security issued by a securitization company, backed by real estate credits, that advances flows and finances the sector via capital markets.

Is it tax-exempt?

Yes, for individual investors — which increases demand and reduces the issuer's funding cost.

Which receivables back it?

Leasing (including built-to-suit), financings, purchase-and-sale contracts and other real estate credits with predictable flow.
