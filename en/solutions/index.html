Solutions

The right instrument for every capital goal.

We structure and manage a full range of capital markets vehicles for agribusiness and real estate — from FIAGRO, FII, CRA and CRI to CPR, debentures and commercial notes — and help you choose what fits your transaction best.

FIAGRO
Agribusiness · Investment fund

Agribusiness production chain funds

FIAGRO (Law 14,130/2021) brought agribusiness closer to capital markets. We structure and manage all three types — Real Estate, Credit Rights and Equity — to invest in land, agri receivables and sector companies.

  • Real Estate, FIDC and Equity types
  • Collateral in land, CPR, CRA and equity
  • Access to qualified and professional investors
View FIAGRO
FII
Real Estate · Investment fund

Real Estate Investment Funds

Equity, credit or hybrid vehicles to monetize developments, generate recurring income and provide investor access. We structure the thesis, bylaws and offering architecture.

  • Income, development, credit and FoF
  • Tax-exempt income for eligible individuals
  • From formation to offering distribution
View FII
CRA
Agribusiness · Securitization

Agribusiness Receivables Certificates

Securitization notes backed by agri receivables. An efficient path for producers, cooperatives and agro-industries to raise capital with competitive cost and longer tenors.

  • Funding across the agribusiness chain
  • Collateral, guarantees and rating structuring
  • Income tax exemption for individuals
View CRA
CRI
Real Estate · Securitization

Real Estate Receivables Certificates

Securitization of real estate receivables to advance cash flows, finance development and enable leasing and built-to-suit operations — freeing capital for new projects.

  • Funding for development, leasing and BTS
  • Collateral, guarantees and fiduciary regime
  • Income tax exemption for individuals
View CRI
